Case study, a qualitative data platform for a mentoring NGO

I led one platform where an NGO's volunteer stories meet its program data, so every level can act on them.

A national NGO mentors children in shelter homes. What volunteers saw each week rarely reached anyone who could act on it. I led the project that changed that.

Three calls I made

A conversation, not a form

Volunteers are undergrads who won't type a long report. So I moved collection to a WhatsApp bot that asks one question at a time, then writes the story for them.

The flow follows the real bot's script, shortened, with names and wording changed.

What I took away

Design the system, not just the screen.

The chat is the small visible part. The real design was who tells whom, what they see back, and how fast help arrives.
