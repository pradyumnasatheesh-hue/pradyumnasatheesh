Case study, Dots by Ooloi Labs with Make A Difference

I led one platform where an NGO's volunteer stories meet its program data, so every level can act on them.

Make A Difference mentors children in shelter homes across India through thousands of young volunteers. What those volunteers saw every week rarely reached anyone who could act on it. I led the project that changed that.

Stories arrive from WhatsApp and land next to the patterns they add up to. Platform screens on this page are recreated from the live product with sample data. The phone is a real screenshot.

Three calls I made

A conversation, not a form

MAD needs stories with real detail. Volunteers are undergrads who won't type a long report. We started with a story form on Dots. I moved collection to a WhatsApp bot that interviews the volunteer, one question at a time, then writes the story for them.

MMAD Storiesonline
The conversation follows the real bot's script, shortened. The child's name is made up, as the bot asks.

One question at a time, after the first test told us off

Tell us what happened, who was there, what changed for the child, what you learned and what you'd do next time.
round one: too much at once, people stalled

What I took away

Design the system, not just the screen.

The chat is the small visible part. The real design was who tells whom, what they see back, and how fast help arrives.
